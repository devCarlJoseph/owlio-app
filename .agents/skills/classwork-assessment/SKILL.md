# Classwork and Assessment

Use for the activity lifecycle in the [educator](../../../OWLIO_EDUCATOR_WORKFLOW_PLAN.md), [ESL teacher](../../../OWLIO_ESL_TEACHER_WORKFLOW_PLAN.md), [tutor](../../../OWLIO_TUTOR_WORKFLOW_PLAN.md), and student [classroom participation](../../../OWLIO_STUDENT__WORKFLOW_PLAN.md) workflows. Keep every activity scoped to its authorized class, lesson, learner/group, or other explicitly supported context.

## Role boundaries

- Educators create and publish official classwork/assessments, review submissions, and provide grades/feedback under the class contract. The Gradebook is a separate view of official results sourced from activities; it must not establish a conflicting grading authority.
- ESL teachers distinguish language practice from assessed/graded work. Skill tags, rubrics, and language levels are only used when explicitly supported; a conversation or temporary whiteboard action does not automatically become a grade or submission.
- Tutors use Activities & Evaluations for tutor-controlled work and feedback. Do not assume school points, grading periods, late rules, auto-grading, or institutional grades.
- Students distinguish personal tasks from educator-assigned classwork. They complete and submit authorized work and see only permitted status and released results; personal completion never edits official activity records.

## Activity types

The product plan distinguishes form-based work (quizzes, tests, exams, practice, review) from non-form work, including file/link submissions, projects and practical/offline activities, discussion-based activities, and reading/content completion. Form question types described include multiple choice/select, true/false, identification, short answer, and essay. Do not imply any type is implemented until verified.

## Workflow and records

Educators create and distribute work, set supported due dates and scoring rules, review submissions, provide grades/evaluations and feedback, and return work where supported. Students complete and submit work and view status/results released to them. The plan's possible lifecycle includes in-progress, submitted, under review, graded, and returned; align statuses to the actual contract rather than assuming this exact state machine.

Objective auto-grading may use educator-defined answer keys and points when supported. Written-response scoring can use rubrics or AI suggestions, but educators retain review and final decision authority. Keep official class records protected and separate from personal student tasks and study-space activity.

Tutor-only Session Notes are not activity feedback and must not be surfaced to learners. A learner-visible feedback/evaluation action must have an explicit audience and follow the role-specific plan.

Use [AI assistance](../ai-assistant/SKILL.md) for generation/evaluation safeguards and [learner progress/reporting](../learner-progress-reporting/SKILL.md) for records and summaries. Never claim a submission, grade, or feedback was saved unless an API confirms it.
