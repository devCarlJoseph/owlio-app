# ESL Teaching

Use for ESL and English-language learning workflows. ESL teachers use the shared educator classroom concepts; language-oriented lesson content does not create a separate authorization role by itself.

## Teaching context

The product plan includes one-to-one and group lessons, conversation practice, grammar, reading, writing, vocabulary, speaking and listening activities, lesson materials, assessments, attendance, feedback, and learner progress. Keep activities attached to the relevant lesson or classroom. Language-specific learner levels (including CEFR where the product chooses to use it) should be represented only according to an approved product/API contract.

## Guidance

- Allow flexible session formats and learner levels; do not assume a school institution, fixed academic calendar, or conventional grading scale.
- Keep educator-authored objectives and learner feedback clear and reviewable.
- AI-generated vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing, or speaking activities and suggested evaluations require educator review and editing before use; final evaluations remain educator-controlled.
- Keep learner-specific assessment and progress private to authorized classroom members.
- Do not claim video meetings, speech analysis, language-level automation, or other integrations unless implemented and contractually supported.
